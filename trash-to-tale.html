CONTEXT

A three-week collaborative residency in Moroni exploring how discarded materials can become characters, stories, music and public encounters.

T

THE CHALLENGE

Create a project that could be artistic, participatory and locally grounded — while working with real production constraints, multiple partners, workshops, public moments and a limited residency timeframe.

THINKING

The process connected material transformation, storytelling, music and community participation. Objects were not treated as decorative waste-art, but as narrative devices carrying place, memory and imagination.
