CONTEXT

How do you introduce a new mobility brand without reducing the experience to a conventional car reveal? The project was developed as a guided showroom journey combining live production, interaction and product discovery.

X

THE CHALLENGE

Build a coherent guest journey around two hero vehicles while keeping the experience fluid, understandable and premium — across arrival, protocol, interactive moments, product discovery and live operations.

THINKING

The experience was structured as a sequence rather than a single reveal. Guests became “Explorers”, received an identity, moved through guided moments and interacted with the brand through a live system, headsets and curated vehicle journeys.
